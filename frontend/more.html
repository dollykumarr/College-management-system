Distance Education

IGNOU — Indira Gandhi National Open University

IGNOU is a central university established by an Act of Parliament in 1985. It offers flexible Open and Distance Learning (ODL), so students can earn a recognised degree through self-learning study material and online resources while working or staying at home, with guidance from the study centre.

Facilities at the Study Centre

  • Counselling sessions with academic counsellors (usually on weekends and holidays)
  • Guidance for admission, re-registration, and assignment submission
  • Access to printed study material and online e-resources
  • Support for term-end examination forms and exam preparation

Programmes Offered

  • Certificate and Diploma programmes
  • Undergraduate programmes such as BA, BCom, BSc, and BCA
  • Postgraduate programmes such as MA, MCom, MSc, MCA, and MBA

Admission & Student Guidelines

  • Admission and re-registration are done online through the official IGNOU website
  • Admissions are generally open in two sessions — July and January
  • Keep your enrolment number and ID card safe and submit assignments before the last date
  • Attend counselling sessions regularly and check the official website for dates and notices

Facility

Library

The college library houses a large collection of books, journals, and digital resources to support the academic and research needs of students and faculty, including dedicated reading zones and a reference section.

Facilities & Resources

  • Extensive collection of textbooks and reference books
  • Academic journals and periodicals
  • Digital/e-resources and online catalog access
  • Quiet reading zones and reference section

Timings

Monday – Saturday: 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Rules & Student Guidelines

  • Maintain silence inside the library at all times
  • Books must be returned within the issued period
  • Library card is mandatory for borrowing books
  • Damage or loss of books is subject to a fine

Reading Room

Facilities Available

  • Peaceful, well-ventilated study environment
  • Individual seating arrangements

Quality Assurance

IQAC — Internal Quality Assurance Cell

The IQAC is responsible for planning, guiding, and monitoring quality-enhancement activities across the institution, ensuring academic and administrative standards stay aligned with national accreditation guidelines.

Objectives

  • Promote a culture of quality in teaching, learning, and administration
  • Encourage continuous self-evaluation and improvement
  • Support the institution's accreditation and assessment processes

Functions & Responsibilities

  • Developing quality benchmarks for academic activities
  • Organizing workshops and training for quality improvement
  • Collecting feedback from students, faculty, and stakeholders
  • Preparing annual quality assurance reports
Co-curricular

NCC — National Cadet Corps

The NCC unit builds discipline, leadership, and a sense of national service among students through structured training, drills, and camps conducted throughout the year.

Activities & Training

  • Regular parade and physical training
  • Annual training camps and adventure activities
  • Leadership and team-building exercises

Benefits & Opportunities

  • Priority consideration in defence services recruitment
  • Certificates recognized for various career opportunities
  • Personality development and discipline
Co-curricular

NSS — National Service Scheme

The NSS unit connects students with community service, encouraging social responsibility through activities such as blood donation camps, cleanliness drives, and welfare programs.

Activities

  • Blood donation and health awareness camps
  • Cleanliness and environmental drives
  • Rural community development programs
  • Special camps and volunteering events

Benefits for Students

  • Develops empathy, leadership, and civic responsibility
  • Certificates that strengthen future applications
  • Hands-on exposure to community development work
