About Ramgarh College

About Ramgarh College, Ramgarh

Established in 1963, Ramgarh College is a Constituent Unit of Vinoba Bhave University, Hazaribag, committed to academic excellence, innovation, and holistic development.

1963

Established

Our Journey

College History

Ramgarh College, Ramgarh was established in the year 1963 with the vision of providing quality higher education to the students of Ramgarh and nearby regions.

Over the decades, the college has developed into one of the leading constituent colleges under Vinoba Bhave University, Hazaribag, offering undergraduate and postgraduate education in Arts, Science, Commerce and Vocational programmes.

The institution continues to promote academic excellence, discipline, innovation, research and holistic development while preparing responsible citizens for society.
